How it works

One tap. Then they just talk.

  1. They tap the orb and talk.

    “I fell in the bathroom.” Their words appear on screen as they speak.

  2. Buddy stays with them.

    One short question at a time. Where they are and what happened, ready to read to 911.

  3. They pick who to call.

    911, a non-emergency line, or you. Nothing is called without their say-so.

Hey Buddy keeps no audio and no recordings. Their phone’s speech service turns their words into text.

Where it stands

What it can’t do yet.

  • It's a prototype. It is not an emergency service. It works alongside 911, not instead of it.
  • Nobody monitors it around the clock.
  • Today they tap to start. They open Hey Buddy and tap the orb, so the phone has to be on and within reach. Fully hands-free “Hey Buddy” (no tap) is not ready on phones yet. It’s what early testers are helping us build.
  • It never calls anyone on its own.
  • Next step: a free 30-day pilot with 5 Vancouver families.
