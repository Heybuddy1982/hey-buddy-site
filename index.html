when something goes wrong, your brain stops working the way it usually does. panic compresses time. decisions feel impossible. heybuddy is built for that exact moment — not to replace 911, but to help you reach it faster and clearer.

how it works

step 1

it hears you

voice-activated. no tapping, no unlocking. works when your hands are full.

step 2

it finds you

it confirms your location and trusted contacts before anything else happens.

step 3

it understands

it classifies what's happening — injury, threat, panic, disorientation — and responds accordingly.

step 4

it asks first

nothing escalates without your say. heybuddy never calls anyone automatically.

built different

governance isn't a feature.
it's the foundation.

most safety apps are built to engage. heybuddy is built to contain — bounded escalation, consent at every step, nothing autonomous.

designed to coexist with emergency services, not compete with them.

no autonomous calls

heybuddy never contacts emergency services on your behalf. you stay in control.

stores nothing

no passive listening. no background monitoring. no data retained after your session ends.

fails safely

if heybuddy isn't sure, it steps back. uncertainty reduces capability — never expands it.

built for institutions

architecture designed to pass legal, compliance, and municipal review — not just app store approval.

heybuddy is launching with focus. each phase adds a lane — same governance, same voice, same consent model.

solo emergencies

for anyone alone when something goes wrong — or anyone who needs both hands free. whether it's an injury, panic attack, threat, or disorientation, heybuddy is fully voice-operated so you can keep applying first aid, supporting someone, or staying mobile while it guides you toward help.
