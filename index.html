The problem

Most technology assumes you can think clearly.

During emergencies, people lose the very abilities required to solve the emergency itself. Stress degrades decision-making, communication, memory, orientation, and situational awareness simultaneously.

People struggle to explain where they are. To communicate what happened. To remember critical information. To operate their phone. To determine what to do next.

How it works

Four steps.
Strict sequence.

Step 01

Activation

The user activates the system using the wake phrase: "Hey Buddy." The system begins validation immediately. Nothing else happens yet.

Step 02

PEAL Validation

Before any action is allowed, the Pre-Execution Admission Layer runs four governance gates. Nothing executes by default. Every activation must earn permission.

Step 03

Structured Assistance

Once validated, Hey Buddy provides bounded assistance appropriate to the situation — orientation, location context, emergency guidance, communication support. All responses remain constrained by governance rules.

Step 04

Controlled Escalation

When appropriate, Hey Buddy assists in contacting trusted contacts or preparing emergency information. Escalation remains governed by consent. The system recommends. The human decides.

Pre-Execution Admission Layer

Nothing executes
by default.

Most AI places intelligence before governance. Hey Buddy reverses that. Every activation passes four gates before execution is permitted. This is not a feature. It is the architecture.

G1

Signal Authenticity

Validates real human speech, replay resistance, environmental consistency, and activation confidence.

Fail → No execution. Passive only.

G2

Identity Confidence

Evaluates trusted speaker confidence, enrolled voice match, and authorized usage thresholds.

Fail → Restricted mode. No escalation.

G3

Intent Classification

Determines emergency, distress, assistance request, accidental activation, or misuse. Only validated crisis intent proceeds.

Fail → Clarification state. Escalation blocked.

G4

Context & Constraint Validation

Checks environmental plausibility, escalation appropriateness, interference conditions, and false activation patterns.

Fail → Hard stop. Log-only mode.

Safety invariants

Rules that
cannot be overridden.

Human Safety Supremacy

No action may increase risk to a human. When uncertainty increases, capability decreases — never expands.

No Autonomous Escalation

The system may recommend. It does not autonomously execute safety-critical escalation. Human authority remains required.

No Risk Downgrading

Escalation cannot automatically reduce itself. Once a crisis state is entered, only authorized human action may reduce escalation severity.

Immutable Accountability

System events and safety actions remain auditable. Critical incident logs may not be silently altered or erased.

No Learning During Crisis

The system does not retrain, experiment, or modify itself during active emergency execution. Learning occurs post-event only.

Governance Supremacy

No model, prompt, agent, or developer may bypass governance constraints. Governance is the operating system — not a feature.
